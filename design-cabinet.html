01Provenance

Wherever the fascinum was worn, envy blinked and looked away.

Two thousand years ago the people of Rome trusted one talisman above all others. They called it the fascinum — sacred to Fascinus, divine guardian of vitality and fortune, whose cult was tended by the Vestal Virgins.

Mothers hung it over cradles. Generals rode to triumph with it fixed beneath their chariots, guarded at the moment envy burned hottest. Its purpose was singular: to break the evil eye — the jealous glance believed to steal health, happiness, and luck.

SOURCE — "HIC HABITAT FELICITAS" / "Here dwells happiness."
Inscribed beside a bakery door, Pompeii. Preserved in ash eighteen centuries. Our name is taken from it.

Is it solid sterling silver?
Yes — each specimen is cast in solid 925 sterling silver, hand-finished with an antique patina and sealed. Not plated. Stamped 925.
Dimensions and chain?
The pendant stands roughly 28 mm — substantial yet comfortable for daily wear. It arrives on a solid sterling chain in your choice of 50 cm or 60 cm.
Will it tarnish?
The patina is intentional and deepens the relief; it is meant to age. Sterling can darken over time — a soft cloth returns the raised surfaces to a bright shine.
